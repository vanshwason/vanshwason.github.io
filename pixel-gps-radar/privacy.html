Pixel GPS Radar Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026

Pixel GPS Radar does not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything it shows is worked out on your watch and stays there.

What the watch face uses

The face reads a few values from your watch to draw the screen. None of them leave the watch.

DataWhy it is usedWhere it goes
Location (your watch's last GPS fix, or your phone's location)To centre the map on where you areStays on the watch. Never sent anywhere.
Heart rate (Instinct models, if switched on)To show your current heart rateStays on the watch
Time and dateTo show the time and dateStays on the watch
Your settings (colour theme, heart rate on or off)To remember your choicesSaved on the watch and in your Garmin Connect app by Garmin's settings system

What it never does

  • It has no internet access. The app requests no network, background or sensor permissions.
  • It shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking.
  • It has no accounts and asks for no name, email or other personal details.
  • It does not sell, rent or share data with anyone, because it holds none to share.

Garmin's role

You install the face through Garmin's Connect IQ Store and the Garmin Connect app. Garmin handles the download, settings sync and your Garmin account under its own privacy policy.

If the face crashes, Garmin may give the developer an error report. It shows the watch model, firmware version and where in the code the error happened, not who you are or where you are.

Changes and contact

If a future version adds a feature that needs data, such as unlocking premium features, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
